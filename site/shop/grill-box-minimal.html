Once it lands

Three Ways to Use It.

On the Grill

Whole rope, coiled, over medium heat, turned once. Peppers and onions on the side.

Sunday Gravy

Brown it in batches, drop it into a pot of sauce, let it go low and slow. Serve over rigatoni.

Sandwich Night

Hot with peppers and onions. Sweet with sauce and Grande. Both on a good roll.

Freezer strategy: portion it into 1 lb bags before it goes in the freezer, and pull what you need for any given dinner.

A few good questions.

Is this a subscription?

No. This is one box for $189, with shipping included. No recurring charges.

What’s the difference between hot and sweet?

Hot has crushed red pepper, paprika, and whole fennel seed. Sweet has cracked black pepper, with no fennel, paprika, or chili heat. Two different family recipes.

How does it arrive?

The sausage ships uncooked and frozen. Everything is labeled and packed in an insulated box with cold packs. See our shipping information.

Are the buns included?

No. Your box includes the sausage and peppers and onions. Bring your own buns, sauce, drinks, and sides. Photos show serving suggestions.
