Once it lands

Three Ways to Cook It.

Sunday gravy for six

Brown all three meats in batches, then drop them into a big pot of simmering San Marzano sauce and let it go low and slow. Serve over rigatoni with bread.

Sandwich night

Hot sausage with peppers and onions on Mancini's. Sweet sausage with sauce and Grande. Meatball with sauce and cheese. Three sandwiches, one family.

On the grill

Whole rope, coiled, over medium heat, turned once. Peppers and onions alongside, a good roll, and that's dinner.

Freezer strategy: portion it into 1 lb bags before it goes in the freezer, and pull what you need for any given dinner. Split across a week it's sausage and peppers Monday, meatball subs Wednesday, sausage pizza Friday.

FAQ

Why is shipping free on this one?

Frozen shipping with cold packs isn't cheap — but on a box this size, it's worth shipping right. We eat the cost rather than tack it on at checkout: it's already built into the $189. The price you see is the price you pay.

Can I substitute?

Not on this bundle — it's a fixed pack. For custom mixes, call the shop at 412-331-9531.

How is it packaged?

Each item is labeled and packed in an insulated box.

What if it thaws in transit?

It shouldn't — but if any of it arrives warm or thawed, email us a photo within 24 hours of delivery and we'll send a free replacement or refund you. No return shipping, no argument. Full policy on our Returns page.

What if I'm not home when it arrives?

Frozen product holds for several hours in the sealed, insulated box. We recommend shipping to an address where someone's around, and you'll get tracking the morning it goes out.
