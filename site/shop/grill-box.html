Before You Order.

What exactly comes in the box?

Ten pounds of Italian sausage — two labeled 5 lb boxes of uncooked rope in natural casing — and one 32 oz bag of peppers and onions. Choose all hot, all sweet, or five pounds of each. Buns, sauce, drinks and sides aren't included. Everything is raw, and it leaves the shop frozen; photos are serving suggestions.

How many people does it feed?

Five pounds of rope feeds about twenty, so ten pounds runs a crowd of roughly forty. Actual yield depends on how you portion it and what else is on the table.

Is this a subscription?

No. One box, $189, shipping included. No recurring charges.

Can I mix it differently?

The three mixes above are what ships at this price. For anything else, call the shop at 412-331-9531 — we'll put together a custom shipped order over the phone.

What's the difference between hot and sweet?

Two different blends, not one recipe with the pepper left in or out. Hot is crushed red pepper, real paprika and whole fennel seed — medium heat that builds on the third bite, not the first. Sweet is cracked black pepper, with no fennel, no paprika and no chili heat. Both are all natural: no fillers, no MSG, no additives, no preservatives.

What if I'm not home when it arrives?

It ships frozen in an insulated box with cold packs. Bring it inside when it arrives and refrigerate or freeze it promptly.

Why is shipping free?

It isn't tacked on at checkout — it's already built into the $189. The price you see is the price you pay.
