Three Ways to Use It.

On the Grill

Whole rope, coiled, over medium heat, turned once. Peppers and onions on the side.

Sunday Gravy

Brown it in batches, drop it into a pot of sauce, let it go low and slow. Serve over rigatoni.

Sandwich Night

Hot with peppers and onions. Sweet with sauce and Grande. Both on a good roll.

Freezer strategy: portion it into 1 lb bags before it goes in the freezer, and pull what you need for any given dinner.
