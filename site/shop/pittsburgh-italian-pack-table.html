Sweet

No fennel · No garlic · No heat

The original 1945 recipe, Abruzzo-style. Mild and savory, seasoned with cracked black pepper.

5 lb in your box

Hot

Whole fennel seed · Medium heat

Calabria-style. Crushed red pepper and real paprika, with heat that builds slowly. The sausage in our sandwich since 1945.

Ingredients: Pork, water, salt, whole fennel seed, garlic, crushed red pepper, black pepper, real paprika, family spice blend. Natural casing.

Does the sweet sausage have fennel?

No. Our sweet has no fennel and no garlic — just pork, salt, black pepper and a touch of dextrose. The fennel is in the hot.

How hot is the hot?

Medium. Crushed red pepper and paprika give it a warmth that builds as you eat. If you want none, pick All Sweet above.

Is it cooked?

No. Both ship uncooked and frozen, as whole rope. Cut it to whatever length you want.
