Questions

Before You Order.

Will it be thawed in time for the game?

It ships frozen and stays that way in transit. Move it to the fridge the day before so it's ready to hit the grill on game day. Order cutoffs and transit times are on the Shipping page.

What if I'm not home when it arrives?

We send every order frozen via two-day service in a sealed, insulated box with cold packs. Bring it inside when it arrives and refrigerate or freeze it promptly.

How long does it keep?

Frozen in the freezer until you're ready to cook it, thawed in the refrigerator — never on the counter. Specific windows are on the Shipping page.

Can I ship it to a different address?

Yes — enter it at checkout. Handy if you're shipping the crate to wherever the tailgate actually is.

Can I choose all hot or all sweet?

This bundle is a fixed pack — sweet, hot, meatballs, and a sausage roll. For a custom mix, call the shop at 412-331-9531.

Why is shipping free?
