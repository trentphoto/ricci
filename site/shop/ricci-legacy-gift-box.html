Questions

FAQ

Can I add a personal note?

Yes — there's a note field at checkout. We hand-write it on a Ricci's card.

Can I ship to a different address?

Yes — set a separate ship-to address at checkout. Great for housewarmings, holidays, condolences.

What if they don't have a freezer?

Most of it goes in the fridge for the week. The bundle is sized for two people to eat through in 5–7 days.

Why is shipping free on this one?

Frozen shipping with cold packs isn't cheap — but on a box this size, it's worth shipping right. We eat the cost rather than tack it on at checkout: it's already built into the $289. The price you see is the price you pay.

What if it thaws in transit?

It shouldn't — but if any of it arrives warm or thawed, email us a photo within 24 hours of delivery and we'll send a free replacement or refund you. No return shipping, no argument. Full policy on our Returns page.

What if I'm not home when it arrives?

Frozen product holds for several hours in the sealed, insulated box. We recommend shipping to an address where someone's around, and you'll get tracking the morning it goes out.
