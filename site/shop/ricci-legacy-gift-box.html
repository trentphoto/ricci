A Week of Italian Dinners.

Sunday gravy

Brown the sausage and meatballs, then drop them into the sauce from the box and let it go low and slow. Serve over rigatoni with bread.

All week long

Sausage and peppers Monday. Meatball subs Wednesday. Stuffed peppers Friday. Sausage pizza Saturday. One box, a week of Italian dinners.

Whole rope, coiled, over medium heat, turned once. Peppers and onions alongside, a good roll, and that's dinner.
