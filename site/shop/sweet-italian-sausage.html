About this product

Classic Sweet Italian Sausage.

Mild, savory, balanced. We make it in small batches — roughly 200 lbs at a time — and the recipe is almost defiantly simple: salt, black pepper, and a touch of dextrose. When the ingredient list is that short, the pork has to be right — which is the whole point. Lean (22–25% fat, well under the federal limit), low sodium, all natural. We hand-stuff every link in McKees Rocks.

Ingredients

Pork, water, salt, black pepper, dextrose. Natural casing. No fennel, no garlic, no fillers, no MSG, no additives, no preservatives.

How to cook it

Three Ways to Cook It.

On the stovetop

Brown it in a heavy pan with a splash of water, covered. Finish uncovered so the casing crisps.

On the grill

Medium heat, turned often, until it's cooked through and the casing has taken some color.

In Sunday gravy

Brown it first, then let it sit in simmering San Marzano sauce. The fat renders into the gravy. That's the whole secret.

On the table

Ideas From the Counter.

Sausage, peppers, and onions on a Mancini's roll. Sliced into pasta with broccoli rabe. Grilled with a side of polenta. Crumbled on pizza. The classics work for a reason.
