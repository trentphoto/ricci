How is it shipped?

Frozen. Each item is labeled and packed in an insulated box with cold packs. Shipping is included in the price shown for your state.

What if it thaws in transit?

If any of it arrives warm or thawed, email us a photo within 24 hours of delivery and we'll send a free replacement or refund you. No return shipping, no argument. Full policy on our Returns page.

When will it arrive?

Order cutoffs and transit times are on the Shipping page. You'll get a tracking number by email when your order ships.

What if I'm not home when it arrives?

We recommend shipping to an address where someone can receive the delivery. Bring the box inside and refrigerate or freeze its contents promptly.
