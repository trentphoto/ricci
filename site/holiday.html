Why pre-order

Here's the deal.

We hand-mix every batch, the same two family blends that came over from Sulmona in 1945. There's only so much we can make in a day, and the week before Thanksgiving is when the whole town wants it at once. Last year that meant an empty case and regulars we had to send home.
