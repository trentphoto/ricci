Why the Line Moves Fast

A Real Lunch Counter, Not a Chain.

Ricci's is a family sausage shop first — the same USDA-inspected kitchen that's been making Italian sausage in McKees Rocks since 1945. Lunch here means the sausage in your sandwich was hand-mixed in this building, in a 200-pound batch, from a family recipe that came over from Sulmona, Italy.

Local trick: call ahead at 412-331-9531 and your order is boxed at the counter when you arrive. In and out in two minutes — plenty of time left on a lunch break from Kennedy, Stowe, Robinson, Crafton, the West End, or downtown Pittsburgh, ten minutes up the road.

Questions

Lunch FAQ.

Can I call ahead?

Please do — 412-331-9531. We'll have it boxed at the counter so you're in and out on a lunch break.

What time should I come for lunch?

We're open from 8:30 AM, so early lunch is no problem. The noon rush is real but the counter moves fast.

Are you open Sunday?

No — Monday through Friday 8:30–6, Saturday 8:30–5, closed Sunday.

Can I grab dinner while I'm in?

That's the move. Fresh sausage by the pound, lasagna, mac 'n cheese, and pasta e' fagioli by the quart — lunch in one hand, dinner in the other.
