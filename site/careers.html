Counter Help — McKees Rocks Shop

We're hiring friendly, reliable counter help for our shop at 590 Pine Hollow Road. You'd be the face people see when they come in for a hot sausage sandwich or a few pounds to take home — greeting customers, taking orders, serving hot food, and ringing folks up. No experience required; we'll teach you everything. A good attitude and showing up on time are what matter most.

What you'd do

  • Greet customers and take orders at the counter and on the phone
  • Serve hot prepared food and weigh out fresh sausage
  • Run the register and help close out the day
  • Keep the counter and front of shop clean and stocked
  • Lend a hand wherever the day needs it — it's a small, busy crew

What we're looking for

  • Friendly, dependable, and on time
  • Comfortable on your feet and working with food
  • Some weekday and Saturday availability
  • 18 or older
  • A little customer-service or food experience is a plus — but not required

The details

  • Part-time or full-time, day shifts (we're open Mon–Sat)
  • Competitive hourly pay, based on experience
  • Lunch on the house and a whole lot of good sausage
  • A family business that treats its people like family

Ricci's is an equal-opportunity employer. We hire on character and work ethic, period.
