What Makes It Ours

Hot, Not Punishing.

Plenty of places in Pittsburgh sell something labeled hot sausage. Ours is the version an Italian grandmother would recognize: heat that shows up on the third bite, not the first, built from crushed red pepper and real paprika — never liquid extract. We hand-mix it in 200-pound batches in the same McKees Rocks building where the Ricci family started in 1945, with whole fennel seed we refuse to buy ground, 22–25% fat, natural casing, and no MSG, fillers, or preservatives.

It's USDA-inspected every day, a standard we've held since 1973 — one of only two USDA-inspected sausage makers in Pittsburgh with a retail counter, and the only one that cooks.

Questions

FAQ

How hot is Ricci's hot sausage?

Medium — it builds. Most people who say they don't like spicy food still finish the sandwich. If you want zero heat, the sweet Italian sausage is the same recipe without the pepper.

Where can I buy it in Pittsburgh?

At our shop — 500 Pine Hollow Road, McKees Rocks, ten minutes from downtown. By the piece from the case or on a sandwich from the counter.

Can I get it delivered?

It ships nationwide, frozen, inside the Pittsburgh Italian Pack. Local lunch and dinner is pickup — call ahead and it's ready when you walk in.
