Media & Features

From CBS Pittsburgh to public television and local radio, Ricci's Italian Sausage has been a fixture in Pittsburgh food media for decades. Ernie Ricci — the self-proclaimed Sausage Prince of McKees Rocks — has told the story of his grandmother's secret recipe more times than he can count. Here are some of our favorites.

WQED Public Television · 2017

NEBBY: "Meat Pittsburgh"

A celebration of interesting places in the Greater Pittsburgh area where you can find meat — farms, butcher shops, restaurants, and small family sausage shops like ours. Ricci's was featured as part of WQED's NEBBY series, funded by a Kickstarter campaign in 2017. "Meat Pittsburgh" is the second program in the series — a love letter to Pittsburgh's meat culture, and we were proud to be a part of it.
