Planning Guide

How much do I need?

  • Sausage: figure 1–2 pieces per person — a large tray (24 pc) covers ~12–18 guests alongside other trays.
  • Meatballs: 2–3 per person as a side, more if they're the main.
  • Lasagna: 12 real servings per tray.
  • Buns: one per sausage — order a dozen per sausage tray and you're covered.

Good to know

  • 24-hour notice on all trays and bread — holidays book up early.
  • Trays come prepared hot (ready to serve) or cooled down (reheat at home).
  • In-store pickup · delivery available on larger Pittsburgh-area orders.
  • Pay at pickup — we confirm every order and final total with you first.
