How It Works

Concept to Case.

01

Tell Us the Concept

The product you have in mind, the flavor direction, and your target price point. A description works. A sample of something you like works better.

02

We Formulate & Sample

We develop a blend or adapt one of ours and send samples for your kitchen or your buyer to taste. We iterate until it's right.

03

Trial Run

We quote production cost, minimum order, and packaging, then run a small first batch. We recommend proving it at small volume before anyone commits to a standing order.

Where We Differentiate

Custom Seasoning, Not a Catalog.

Most co-packers hand you a SKU list. We build the formulation around what you're trying to serve or sell — a house Italian tuned to a specific sandwich, a beer-brat blend built to pair with a taproom's flagship, a retail blend that has to survive a grocery cold case and still taste like something.

The spice blend Ernest Ricci brought from Sulmona, Italy in 1945 is still hand-mixed in this building. Eighty years of doing that is the skill we bring to developing yours.

Who It's For

Three Kinds of Buyer.

Restaurants

A house sausage on the menu under your own name, consistent batch to batch, on a standing delivery schedule. Seasonal one-offs for LTOs and festivals too.

Breweries & Taprooms

A brat or sausage built around your beer, produced for release events, taproom kitchens, and packaged retail sales.

Brands & Distributors

A finished, USDA-inspected, retail-legal SKU with compliant labeling — sellable across state lines without building your own plant.

Labeling & Compliance

Legal to Sell, Out of the Box.

We can produce compliant labels — ingredient list in descending order of weight, allergen statement, net weight, and your business name and address — for an added fee. Or you supply your own pre-approved labels and we apply them.

Because production runs under federal inspection, the finished product carries the USDA mark of inspection and can be sold across state lines, into retail, and to wholesale accounts.
