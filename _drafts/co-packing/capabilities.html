Questions

Co-Packing FAQ.

Do you sign NDAs for proprietary recipes?

Yes. A recipe you bring stays yours — we don't retain, reuse, or share it.

Can I bring my own meat?

Talk to us first. Raw material received into a federally inspected establishment has to come from another inspected source and carries its own receiving, records, and HACCP requirements. It's often simpler and cheaper for us to source it. [TK — confirm policy with FSIS Philadelphia District Office before publishing]

Do you process livestock for farms?

We don't slaughter and we don't do cut-and-wrap. If your hogs have already been harvested at an inspected establishment, talk to us about turning the trim into sausage under your farm's own label. [TK — confirm this is operationally feasible before publishing]

Can I start with a small trial run?

Yes, and we recommend it. Prove the product at low volume before committing to a standing order.

How consistent is the product across runs?

Same formulation, same building, same people, under daily federal inspection. Consistency batch to batch is the reason Pittsburgh kitchens have run our sausage for decades.

Do you work with companies outside Pennsylvania?

Yes — our grant of inspection is federal, so product can ship interstate. Logistics are worked out case by case. [TK — confirm freight approach]

How do I get started?

Fill out our co-packing interest form or call 412-331-9531 with your product, rough volume, and timeline. We respond within one business day.
