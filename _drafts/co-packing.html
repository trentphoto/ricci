The Grant of Inspection Is the Whole Point.

A USDA grant of inspection is slow and expensive to obtain and nearly impossible to stand up from scratch. Without one, product can't be sold across state lines, into most retail, or to wholesale accounts. Ricci's has held ours continuously since 1973, inspected in this building, and the USDA has recognized this establishment nationally for sanitation and product compliance.

Why Ricci's

Eighty Years in One Building.

Most co-packers hand you a catalog and ask which SKU you want. We build the formulation around what you're actually trying to sell. The spice blend that came over from Sulmona, Italy in 1945 is still hand-mixed here, and the same skill that made it is what goes into developing yours — a house Italian for a kitchen, a beer-brat blend for a taproom, a retail SKU that has to hold up on a shelf.

We're small enough to run a trial batch before you commit and old enough to hold a spec batch after batch. Pittsburgh kitchens and markets have run our sausage for decades on exactly that basis.
